Marketing Case Study

FullProof: a career portfolio, built while you work

A mock B2B SaaS campaign: content strategy, platform adaptation, and copywriting, developed as a portfolio piece for a Junior Social Media Specialist application.

"The burden of proof is on us, not you."
The Brief

Where the idea came from

The problem

Two years into a corporate internship, I had no organized record of the work I'd actually done: presentations, research, campaigns, partly because I hadn't kept track, and partly because the work itself belonged to the company.

The concept

FullProof is a mock HR/L&D tool that automatically builds an employee's career portfolio as they work, without ever exposing confidential company material, solving the exact problem I lived through.

Campaign

One week, five beats

A five-day content calendar built as a narrative arc: trigger, confrontation, hinge, callback, reveal, rather than five unrelated posts.

Proving your work shouldn't feel like a second job. You already did the hard part: the projects, the late nights, the problems you solved when no one else could. Somewhere along the way, keeping a record of it became its own exhausting task on top of the real one. It doesn't have to stay that way.
